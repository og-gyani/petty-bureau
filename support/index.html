Quick answers

  • Is my data uploaded anywhere? No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
  • Notifications don't arrive. Check Settings > Apps > Petty Bureau > Notifications, and that Focus isn't silencing them.
  • A widget button does nothing. Open the app once after installing or updating, then try the widget again.
  • Nicknames like "the Friday text" don't appear. They need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. The jokes work without them.
  • How do I delete everything? Delete the app. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
